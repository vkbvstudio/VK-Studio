PROJECT POLICY

Clear expectations make better projects.

50% non-refundable downpayment required. The project will only be scheduled and started after the downpayment has been received.

The remaining 50% is due before final delivery or website handover. Additional requests outside the agreed scope may require an additional fee.

Timelines are estimates and may vary depending on project complexity, client response time, revision rounds, and the volume of requested work.
